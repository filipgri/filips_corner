Hi.

I’m Filip, a Design Researcher in London specialising in accessibility. I design with - not just for - people. I study GenAI, I deploy it, but I’m cautious and critical. I work with very diverse groups of people with disabilities. Just to get a sense - my projects span neurodiverse audience, from people with learning disabilities, people on the spectrum to adult tech users ie., post-stroke survivors living with aphasia. I study Communication as I believe it sits at the heart everyday technology use - How do you say you are in pain when you have no verbal abilities to say so. How do you watch TV when you do not understand the language spoken? How do you inform youself about the world when you comprehension ability is disrupted? How we change content and technology to help people better absorb everyday information and communication back? How do you apply for tax benefits when the system is overwhelming? How do you apply for Gov benefits when you foundational skills (typing, reading) are impaired?
