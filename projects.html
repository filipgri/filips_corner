"Computer Says No" - Disability Benefits under AI Governance

Applying for Government Disability Benefits is a complex process …

Applying for Goverment Disability Benefits is a complex process, extremely important but inaccessible to many. This is still an ongoing project and we are trying to disentagle this system with 40 people living with aphasia and offer more meaningful engangement. So far, we know that the current system contrains peoples' agency to contest top-down automatisation changes (ie., RoboCall). Most of these barriers are deeply embedded in digital infrastructures: denial of support, dehumanising assessments and prolongued burocratic limbo. The workload placed on applicants routinely exceeds cognitive and energy budgets, breaching basic fairness, accessibility, and equal opportunity. Transparency is weak. People reject the idea that AI will seamlessy resolve these matters as current AI models have sharp knowledge boundaries. WATCH THIS SPACE!!!
